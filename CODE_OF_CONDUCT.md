# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As a hackathon team, we are committed to making collaboration a respectful, productive, and inclusive experience for every member.

## Standards

**Expected:**
- Respectful and constructive code reviews
- Clear communication when blocked
- Acknowledging others' contributions in PRs
- Raising blockers within 12 hours (per CONTRIBUTING.md)

**Not acceptable:**
- Dismissing teammates' work without constructive feedback
- Committing directly to `main` (bypassing review)
- Blaming individuals for pipeline failures — we debug together
- Ghosting the team during sprint periods

## Enforcement

Issues can be raised with the team lead (Shardul) privately. Given our September 4 deadline, unresolved conduct issues will be escalated to the team group immediately.
